Block Shelf – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Block Shelf: Wood Block Puzzle (Android package com.shazaib.blockshelf), published by Shazaib Sarwar ("we", "us"), handles information. Block Shelf is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. It shows ads and offers one optional in-app purchase ("Remove Ads").

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or receive any personal information. We run no servers and no analytics for this game.

Your game progress (best score, saved game, level stars) and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language and colour theme) are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Reset progress deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Block Shelf shows ads from Google AdMob: banner ads, interstitial ads between rounds, and optional rewarded video ads (for example to get a hint or extra moves). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID or the iOS Advertising Identifier
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, clicks) and diagnostic data such as crash logs

Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchase

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We never see or store your payment details. The store only tells the app whether the purchase is owned, so ads can be turned off, and it can be restored on a new device.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): Block Shelf asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can decline personalised ads and still play the full game, and you can change your choice later from the privacy options entry in Settings when it is shown.
  • Tracking on iOS: on iPhone and iPad the app asks for permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency before any tracking. You can say no, or change it later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices).
  • Remove ads: buying "Remove Ads" stops banner and pop-up ads.
  • Offline play: the game works fully without an internet connection, and no ads load while you are offline.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the ad data processed by Google AdMob described above, no data leaves your device.

Children

Block Shelf is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through ads, contact us and we will help you ask Google to remove it.

Security and retention

Game progress stays on your device under the operating system's app sandbox. Google keeps ad data according to its own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
